## How to post (both platforms)

1. First line is the hook. Everything after is optional.
2. One CTA. Never stack "download" + "follow" + "comment" + "share".
3. Hashtags live in the first comment on Instagram. On TikTok they sit at the
   end of the caption, 3–5 max.
4. Pin a question as the first comment so people have something easy to reply
   to. Replies feed the algorithm more than likes.
5. Post Screenshot first (the demo), Dry Texter second (the want), How It
   Works third (the install). That is the funnel.

## What not to post

- Do not claim App Store. This is Google Play only.
- Do not say "unlimited free" or "always free". End cards say **free to try**,
  matching the keyboard's remaining-reply chip.
- Do not show AI replies the model would not actually write. Comments will
  call it out.
- Do not stack 20 hashtags in the caption. First comment on IG, 3–5 on TikTok.
- Do not post a silent file. Instagram throttles reels with no audio track.
